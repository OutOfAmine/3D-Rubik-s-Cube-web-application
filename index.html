How to play

Look around. Drag the gray background to orbit, scroll to zoom, or use the arrow buttons bottom‑left.

Turn a layer. Drag a sticker in the direction you want that layer to move, or use the buttons.

Notation. U up, D down, L left, R right, F front, B back. Plain = clockwise looking at that face, ' = counter‑clockwise, 2 = half turn. Yellow is up, white is down, orange front.

Stuck? Hint shows the next move and which stage of the beginner method you are in. Apply hint performs it. Solve all plays the whole solution; Step plays it one move at a time. Explain lists every stage with its moves.

Beginner method (layer by layer):

  • White cross on the bottom
  • White corners (first layer)
  • Middle layer edges
  • Yellow cross, then yellow face (orientation)
  • Position top corners, then top edges
