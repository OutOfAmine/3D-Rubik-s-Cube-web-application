How to play

  1. Tap Play. The cube scrambles and the timer starts.
  2. Drag a sticker in the direction you want the layer to move. Drag the background to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  3. Stuck? Tap Hint for the next move, Solve to watch the cube solve itself, or Explain for the whole plan, stage by stage.

Notation: U up, D down, L left, R right, F front, B back. ' counter-clockwise, 2 half turn. Yellow up, white down, orange front.
